Privacy Policy

This policy describes how VEX AI may handle information when you use the website. This template should be reviewed and adjusted to match the actual deployment and applicable law.

Information collected

Depending on your use, information may include your email address, profile details, messages, chat history, and files or images you choose to upload.

How information is used

Information may be used to authenticate accounts, provide chat and saved-history features, respond to requests, troubleshoot issues, and protect the service against misuse.

AI processing and providers

Messages and relevant attachments may be sent to configured providers, including Supabase for authentication and storage and Groq for AI responses. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy practices. Avoid submitting passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information.

Storage and retention

Account and conversation data may be stored by configured services and retained until deleted or no longer needed, subject to backups, technical limitations, and legal obligations. Retention depends on the actual database and provider settings.

Sharing

VEX AI does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the service or disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights and security.

Security

Reasonable safeguards should be used, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and requests

You can stop using the service at any time. Depending on deployed features, you may be able to delete chats or request account/data deletion. Contact the project operator through the official repository for privacy questions.

Children

The service is not intended for children below the minimum age required under applicable law.

Changes and contact

This policy may change as the service changes. For questions, contact the VEX AI project operator through the project repository.
