Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how VEX AI handles account details, saved conversations, prompts, attachments, and information sent to service providers. Supabase handles account authentication and saved-chat records. Chat requests and relevant attachments are sent to Groq to generate responses. When web lookup is triggered, search queries may be sent to a configured search provider and public URLs in your message may be fetched.

1. Information Collected

Depending on deployed features, information may include account identifiers, email, profile details, prompts, responses, chat history, and uploaded files.

2. Account Data

Supabase Authentication handles sign-in and session validation. Your username is read from the profiles table when available; otherwise the app uses account metadata or your email prefix as a display name.

3. Conversation Content

VEX AI sends your message and up to 12 recent conversation entries to the chat function for context. Saved messages are stored in the Supabase conversations table under your account.

4. Uploaded Files

Attachments are processed when you choose to submit them.

5. Authentication

Supabase may authenticate accounts and validate sessions.

6. AI Provider Processing

Your message, recent conversation context, and relevant attached image data are sent to Groq's API to generate a response.

7. Search Queries

When a message appears to request current information or web search, VEX AI attempts a search using Tavily when configured, with DuckDuckGo as a fallback.

8. Public URL Fetching

VEX AI may fetch up to two public HTTP or HTTPS URLs in your message and extract HTML or plain text. Common local and private network addresses are skipped.

9. Service Operations

Information may be used to operate, maintain, and troubleshoot the service.

10. Security

11. Legal Compliance

16. Retention

17. Chat History

Saved chats remain in the Supabase database until removed under the database configuration. The application code does not set a fixed retention period; provider logs and backups may follow separate retention rules.

18. Deletion Requests

19. Account Deletion
