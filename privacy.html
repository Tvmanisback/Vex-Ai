Privacy

This page explains the main services used by VEX AI and practical steps to protect your account.

Account authentication

VEX AI uses Supabase authentication to handle sign-in and account sessions. Your account credentials should only be entered into the official VEX AI sign-in interface.

AI conversations

When you send a message, its content is sent to the AI service used to generate a response. Do not send passwords, payment details, private keys, authentication links, or other sensitive information.

AI limitations

AI-generated responses may be inaccurate, incomplete, or out of date. Check important information independently before acting on it.

Keeping your account safe

  • Use a strong, unique password.
  • Do not share account access or confirmation links.
  • Be cautious when using a shared device; sign out when finished.

This is a plain-language product notice, not a legal guarantee. Service providers may process data according to their own policies and your account configuration.
