Troubleshooting

Try the relevant steps below. If a problem persists, report the exact error text without sharing secrets.

The app is blank or white

  1. Reload the page.
  2. Open VEX AI in a private/incognito tab.
  3. Clear cached website data and reload.
  4. If it remains blank, the published site files may not be loading correctly. This is a site publishing issue rather than an account problem.

I can't create an account

  • Check the email address and password.
  • If asked to confirm your email, follow the confirmation link first.
  • If the email is already registered, return to the login screen.

Google sign-in fails

Google sign-in must be enabled for VEX AI, and the site's address must be allowed as a redirect URL by the authentication provider. If you are redirected to an error, record the exact message.

VEX does not reply

  • Check your internet connection and retry.
  • Reload the page and sign in again if needed.
  • The AI service may be unavailable or may have reached a usage limit.
  • Save the error wording when reporting the issue.

How to report a problem

Include what you were doing, what happened instead, the exact error message, and your browser/device. Never send passwords, sign-in links, tokens, or API keys.
